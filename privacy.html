Privacy Policy

Immaculate Entertainment

Last updated: March 2026

SABOR ("the App") is operated by Immaculate Entertainment. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Email address when you create an account (optional).
  • Usage data: Search queries, restaurants viewed, features used, and app interactions.
  • Location data: Your approximate location when you grant permission, used to show nearby restaurants.
  • Device information: Device model, operating system version, and app version.
  • Purchase information: Subscription and purchase history, processed securely through Apple and RevenueCat.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and personalize restaurant recommendations
  • Improve our AI search and discovery features
  • Process subscription payments and manage your account
  • Send relevant communications (only if you opt in)
  • Analyze app performance and fix issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Location Data

We only access your location when you explicitly grant permission. Location is used solely to show nearby restaurants and personalize recommendations. You can revoke location permission at any time through your device settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Service providers: Firebase (authentication, database), RevenueCat (purchases), Google Places (restaurant data), and Anthropic (AI recommendations).
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile section of the app, which will remove your personal data from our systems.

6. Children's Privacy

SABOR is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Third-Party Services

SABOR uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google Places API — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Anthropic Claude AI — anthropic.com/privacy

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
